Each member is uniquely identified to HealthLX by this element:

`roster/member/unique_person_ids/unique_person_id`

HealthLX requires the `unique_person_id` value as the unique and permanent identifier for the patient across all submissions, regardless of other identifying information that is subject to change (such as the member ID used in various plans).

The complex `roster/member/member_identity` element was formerly noted as required, but it is not required in version 6.1. HealthLX does not use these values.
